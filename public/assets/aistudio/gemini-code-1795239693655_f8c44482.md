### 2. What Is Correctly Implemented (Preserve Intact)

- **OCR connection & fusion pipeline:**
  - `ScreenCaptureProjectionService.kt`: Captures the game screen.
  - `ScreenOcrAnalyzer.kt`: Uses ML Kit to recognize text, controls, stake, countdown, and multiplier, with checks for confidence, stale frames, and conflicting readings.

  - `AutomationEngine.kt`: Uses fresh Accessibility targets to execute actions.
  - **Important limitation to preserve accurately:** OCR is supplementary—it helps read stake, Auto Cash Out values, countdown, phase, and multiplier, but intentionally does **not** create clickable `BET` or `CASH OUT` targets. Button clicks still depend on Accessibility detection, and OCR runs through the screen-capture projection service (requiring permission and start-up; not automatically guaranteed active the moment monitoring starts).
- **Unified hand and settings:**
  - `FloatingHandsOverlayManager.kt`: Single hand switching between `BET` and `CASH OUT`, with double-tap and long-press opening settings.
  - `OverlaySettingsPanel.kt`: Separate `BET` and `CASH OUT` sections backed by shared `StrategyConfig`.

## Priority Repair Order & Exact Requirements

Execute repairs in this order:

### Priority 1: Exact Button Identity, Bounds Containment & Collision Protection
**File:** `app/src/main/java/com/example/service/AccessibilityController.kt`
- **Eliminate BET vs. CASH OUT asymmetry:** `BET` currently checks whether a clickable node intersects detected `BET` bounds, whereas `CASH OUT` can match keywords (`cash out`, `claim`, `collect`) and call `ACTION_CLICK` without verifying that the node's screen bounds match the verified `CASH OUT` button—risking clicks on wrong controls with matching labels or oversized parent containers.
- **Fix `Rect.intersects()` weakness:** Overlapping rectangles do not prove the clickable node is the exact intended button.
- **Required behavior for BOTH `BET` and `CASH OUT`:**
  - Verify **exact target identity**, **bounds containment** (not mere intersection), **button role**, **package**, **enabled/clickable state**, and **collision-free screen area** immediately before every `ACTION_CLICK`.
  - For `CASH OUT` collision checks, explicitly account for `BET`, `CANCEL`, and other nearby controls.
  - Verify screen bounds and exact clickable node geometry before dispatching either action.

### Priority 2: Mandatory Stake Verification When Enabled
**File:** `app/src/main/java/com/example/data/engine/AutomationEngine.kt`
- Currently, post-stake and pre-`BET` checks only reject a mismatch when `detectedStakeText != null`, allowing a missing/null stake reading to bypass the final read-back check when verification is enabled.
- **Required behavior:** When stake verification is enabled, a missing (`null` or blank) stake reading must be treated as **unverified** (fail closed / block `BET`), **never** accepted by omission. Require the exact configured stake to be confirmed immediately before clicking `BET`. Make missing stake readings fail safely.

### Priority 3: Strict `BET` Acceptance Verification
**File:** `app/src/main/java/com/example/data/engine/AutomationEngine.kt`
- Currently, after clicking `BET`, the engine may fall back to the previous game state when a fresh scan returns `null`, risking a stale `BET_QUEUED` or `CASH_OUT_AVAILABLE` state being mistaken for confirmation of the new bet.
- **Required behavior:** Require a **successful fresh post-click scan** showing a **valid state transition for the same game and round**. If the post-click scan is missing/`null` or stale, treat the bet as **unconfirmed** (fail safely) rather than falling back to prior state.

### Priority 4: Strict `CASH OUT` Authorization & Positive Confirmation
**File:** `app/src/main/java/com/example/service/AutomationController.kt`
- Currently:
  1. `cashOutAllowed` permits `SiteButtonState.UNKNOWN`.
  2. Post-click logic treats any state other than `CASH_OUT_AVAILABLE`—including `UNKNOWN`—as successful.
- **Required behavior:**
  - `SiteButtonState.UNKNOWN` must **never** authorize `CASH OUT` and must **never** confirm `CASH OUT` success.
  - Require a **confirmed active bet** before attempting `CASH OUT`.
  - Require **positive post-action confirmation** (such as a verified game state transition, confirmed payout/balance change, or explicit game confirmation). If positive confirmation is unavailable, report **`UNCONFIRMED`**.

### Priority 5: Accurate Win / Loss Settlement
**File:** `app/src/main/java/com/example/data/engine/AutomationEngine.kt`
- In `settleActiveRoundBet()`, `val isWin = cashedOutInFlight || crashedMultiplier >= config.targetMultiplier` currently treats a crash at or above `config.targetMultiplier` as a win even when `CASH OUT` was not confirmed (for example, target `2x`, multiplier reaches `2.5x`, `CASH OUT` click fails, and round crashes). This corrupts P&L records, strategy performance, progression decisions, historical win rates, and strategy analysis.
- **Required behavior:** Only a **positively confirmed Cash Out** counts as a win. Reaching `targetMultiplier` before or during a crash is **not** proof of a Cash Out and must not be recorded as a win.

### Priority 6: Game Detection, OCR Anchoring & Capture/Lifecycle Hardening
Update the remaining components so detection, OCR, and lifecycle boundaries are consistent and race-free:
- **`app/src/main/java/com/example/service/ScreenEarnerAccessibilityService.kt`:**
  - Distinguish **browser detected** from **supported game detected**. Do not mark a game detected merely because Chrome or another browser package is active without a confirmed supported game adapter.
  - Ensure `isReady` requires sufficient game-specific signals, reliable screen controls, configured minimum detection confidence, and consistent handling of stale data and OCR uncertainty.
- **`ScreenOcrAnalyzer.kt`:**
  - Keep OCR supplementary (no independent clickable target creation), and improve **Auto Cash Out** and **stake numeric anchoring** so unrelated numbers on screen are not misread.
- **`ScreenCaptureProjectionService.kt`:**
  - Ensure capture and OCR lifecycle recovery works cleanly across **permission loss**, **service restart**, and **screen rotation**.
- **`ScreenColorSampler.kt`:**
  - Ensure stopping capture prevents an old in-flight OCR callback from publishing stale results after stop.

### Priority 7: Expand Test Coverage
**File:** `VerifiedAutomationAndOcrTest.kt`
- Add tests covering every failure and safety scenario, including:
  - Wrong-node clicks and keyword/container mismatches on `CASH OUT`
  - Out-of-bounds targets and nearby control collisions (`BET`, `CANCEL`, sibling controls)
  - Missing/null stake text when stake verification is enabled
  - Missing/null or stale post-click scans on `BET`
  - `SiteButtonState.UNKNOWN` during `CASH OUT` authorization and post-click confirmation (`UNCONFIRMED` when unconfirmed)
  - Win/loss accounting when multiplier reaches/exceeds `targetMultiplier` without a confirmed Cash Out (must be a loss, not a win)
  - Browser-only detection vs. supported game detection and `isReady` gating
  - Stale in-flight callbacks after stopping capture
